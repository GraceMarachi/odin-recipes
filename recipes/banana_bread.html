Banana Bread Recipe

Description

This easy banana bread recipe is a delicious way to use up ripe bananas. It is soft, moist, and perfect for breakfast or a snack.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • ¼ teaspoon salt
  • ¾ cup brown sugar
  • ½ cup butter
  • 2 large eggs, beaten
  • 2 ⅓ cups mashed overripe bananas

Steps

  1. Prepare the ingredients and oven.
    Gather everything you need, preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C), and lightly grease a 9 × 5-inch loaf pan.
  2. Make the batter.
    In one bowl, mix the flour, baking soda, and salt.
    In another bowl, beat the brown sugar and butter until smooth. Add the eggs and mashed bananas, then combine this mixture with the dry ingredients.
    Stir just until everything is incorporated. Avoid overmixing.
  3. Transfer the batter.
    Pour the finished batter into the greased loaf pan and spread it evenly
  4. Bake the bread.
    Place the pan in the preheated oven and bake for approximately 60 minutes.
    Check for doneness by inserting a toothpick into the center; it should come out clean or with only a few crumbs.
  5. Cool before serving.
    Allow the banana bread to rest in the pan for about 10 minutes.
    Carefully remove it and place it on a wire rack to cool completely before slicing.
