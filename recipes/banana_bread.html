Banana Bread Recipe

Description

This easy banana bread recipe is a delicious way to use up ripe bananas. It is soft, moist, and perfect for breakfast or a snack.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • ¼ teaspoon salt
  • ¾ cup brown sugar
  • ½ cup butter
  • 2 large eggs, beaten
  • 2 ⅓ cups mashed overripe bananas
